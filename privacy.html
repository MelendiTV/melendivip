Privacy Policy

This page explains how Melendi VIP Promotions LLC collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, hotel details, reservation details, and any additional information you provide through our booking or contact forms.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to coordinate reservations, communicate with you, arrange transportation assistance, process booking requests, improve service quality, and respond to customer inquiries.

3. Payment Processing

Online payments are processed through third-party payment providers. Melendi VIP Promotions does not store full credit card details on its website.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary details only with trusted service providers or partner venues when required to complete your requested reservation coordination or related service.

5. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use basic analytics, cookies, or similar technologies to improve site functionality, measure traffic, and enhance the user experience.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact Melendi VIP Promotions through the contact information listed on our website.
